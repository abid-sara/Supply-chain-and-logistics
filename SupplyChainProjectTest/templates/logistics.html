Optimizing Logistics and Transportation

This project focuses on enhancing logistics and transportation efficiency to minimize delivery time, reduce costs, and ensure timely availability of goods.

We will specifically address the challenges in Algeria, considering the map of the country and recent agricultural production cycles (e.g., wheat, potatoes, dates, tomatoes, citrus) to optimize transportation routes.

Our objectives include:

  • Mapping out trucking capacities and available fleets between cities.
  • Defining an optimization function considering distance, seasonality, and truck capacities.
  • Implementing search strategies such as A*, BFS, UCS, and Hill Climbing to evaluate optimal delivery schedules.
  • Comparative analyses to suggest the best fleet assignments for transporting various products according to seasonal demands.
